# Team and user management

Invite staff and give them the access required for their role.

## Availability and permissions

Open **Settings → Apps** and find **Team and user management**. The card's availability, price, connection state and available actions apply to your account. A help article does not activate an app. Ask the owner to review missing access; staff permissions and app availability are separate. Do not use another person's login to bypass a restriction.

## Set up

1. As the owner, open the Team/User management app and review its pricing and account limits.
2. Invite or create the staff member using their own contact details and assign relevant calendars and permissions.
3. Verify the staff account can access the intended workflows and cannot access unrelated clients, records or financial settings.

## Everyday use

Maintain permissions when responsibilities change and remove access when staff leave. Attendance viewing, check-in, credential management and corrections may require different permissions.

## Troubleshooting

If a staff member cannot see a feature, check app activation, role permissions and calendar/client access separately. Do not solve missing access by sharing the owner account.

## Limits and changes

Membership plan management and some provider settings are owner-only. Adding a staff member may affect billing or staff allowances; check Subscription settings before increasing capacity.

## Related help

Return to [App help](https://help.practize.io/help/) to find related setup guides. Before changing a connected workflow, finish pending work and verify its status in the relevant booking, invoice or client record.
