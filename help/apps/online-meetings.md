# Online meetings

Add meeting links and joining notes to supported appointments.

## Availability and permissions

Open **Settings → Apps** and find **Online meetings**. The card's availability, price, connection state and available actions apply to your account. A help article does not activate an app. Ask the owner to review missing access; staff permissions and app availability are separate. Do not use another person's login to bypass a restriction.

## Set up

1. Activate Online meetings and open settings.
2. Set the default online meeting URL and notes for each applicable calendar.
3. Save and inspect an appointment and its client communication to confirm the correct link is used.

## Everyday use

Review meeting details before sending the appointment to the client. Update defaults when the practice changes its meeting service.

## Troubleshooting

If a client receives an incorrect link, check the appointment details and calendar-specific default. Confirm that the external meeting itself is available and accessible.

## Limits and changes

Practize stores the configured meeting link; this does not prove that a video room was created or that the provider is connected. Do not place reusable private-host credentials in client notes.

Return to [App help](https://help.practize.io/help/) to find related setup guides. Before changing a connected workflow, finish pending work and verify its status in the relevant booking, invoice or client record.
