# Online Booking

Publish the services and available times clients can book online.

## Availability and permissions

Open **Settings → Apps** and find **Online Booking**. The card's availability, price, connection state and available actions apply to your account. A help article does not activate an app. Ask the owner to review missing access; staff permissions and app availability are separate. Do not use another person's login to bypass a restriction.

## Set up

1. Activate Online Booking and open its settings. Review the booking address, headline, description and displayed contact information.
2. Configure bookable calendars and services, availability and the client details required to make a booking.
3. Review login, multiple-service selection and external confirmation settings where shown. If Google Calendar is connected, review whether busy events block times.
4. Preview the public page as a client and verify service selection, available times and confirmation. Save website appearance separately where the page provides a publication flow.

## Everyday use

Share the public booking link or use the website button options. Manage resulting bookings in the calendar. Check availability after changing opening hours, services or staff assignments.

## Troubleshooting

No times may mean a calendar or service is not bookable, opening hours do not cover the requested day, or busy bookings block the interval. Check the actual calendar rather than only the website design. A payment error is separate from page publication.

## Limits and changes

Changing the public Home appearance does not change reservation or login rules. Online payment requires its own ready provider connection. Do not publish private client information in public descriptions.
