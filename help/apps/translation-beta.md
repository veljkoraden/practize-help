# Translation beta

A legacy translation contribution workflow exists in the codebase but its Apps activation surface is disabled.

## Availability and permissions

Open **Settings → Apps** and find **Translation beta**. The card's availability, price, connection state and available actions apply to your account. A help article does not activate an app. Ask the owner to review missing access; staff permissions and app availability are separate. Do not use another person's login to bypass a restriction.

## Set up

1. Do not look for an enabled Translation beta activation card; the current Apps page explicitly hides that flow.
2. Use the normal language options available to your account instead.
3. Report incorrect wording to support with the page and phrase, without including private client information.

## Everyday use

If a translated phrase is missing, the interface may use its configured fallback language. A fallback does not mean the underlying feature is unavailable.

## Troubleshooting

Do not use old direct URLs or hidden modal controls to enable translation beta. Contact support if the normal language selector is not working.

## Limits and changes

No public availability, translation-completeness guarantee or contribution review deadline is promised by this legacy entry.

## Related help

Return to [App help](https://help.practize.io/help/) to find related setup guides. Before changing a connected workflow, finish pending work and verify its status in the relevant booking, invoice or client record.
