# EAN electronic invoicing

Prepare supported EAN/OIOUBL invoicing details for eligible customers.

## Availability and permissions

Open **Settings → Apps** and find **EAN electronic invoicing**. The card's availability, price, connection state and available actions apply to your account. A help article does not activate an app. Ask the owner to review missing access; staff permissions and app availability are separate. Do not use another person's login to bypass a restriction.

## Set up

1. Activate EAN where offered for the practice and open its settings.
2. Review the sender and electronic-invoice settings, including the available personal-identification-number option.
3. On the intended customer/invoice, enter the required recipient and reference details accurately.
4. Validate the invoice information and use the supported electronic sending action. Check its actual result.

## Everyday use

Keep the recipient identifier and invoice reference aligned with the customer requirements. Inspect the invoice after electronic submission.

## Troubleshooting

If submission fails, correct the specific missing or invalid field and check whether the invoice was already transmitted before resending.

## Limits and changes

Electronic submission is not proof of payment. Do not invent recipient identifiers or include unnecessary personal identification data. Country eligibility and receiving-system requirements apply.
