# Automatic cleanup

Identify inactive client data for the cleanup workflow configured by the practice.

## Availability and permissions

Open **Settings → Apps** and find **Automatic cleanup**. The card's availability, price, connection state and available actions apply to your account. A help article does not activate an app. Ask the owner to review missing access; staff permissions and app availability are separate. Do not use another person's login to bypass a restriction.

## Set up

1. Open the app setup as an authorized user and read the described selection and cleanup rules.
2. Choose the inactivity or retention criteria exposed by your account. Review which records are affected before enabling automation.
3. Confirm the practice retention policy and review any available preview or warning before saving.

## Everyday use

Review cleanup results and investigate unexpected inclusions before widening the rules. Keep the selection criteria aligned with the practice policy.

## Troubleshooting

If expected records remain, inspect the configured inactivity criteria and exclusions. Do not shorten a retention period just to clear an error or remove a record faster.

## Limits and changes

Cleanup can affect retained information. This guide does not authorize deletion or supply a legal retention period. App activation alone is not a backup or a restoration guarantee.

## Related help

Return to [App help](https://help.practize.io/help/) to find related setup guides. Before changing a connected workflow, finish pending work and verify its status in the relevant booking, invoice or client record.
