# Gift cards

Configure gift cards sold through the supported online purchase flow.

## Availability and permissions

Open **Settings → Apps** and find **Gift cards**. The card's availability, price, connection state and available actions apply to your account. A help article does not activate an app. Ask the owner to review missing access; staff permissions and app availability are separate. Do not use another person's login to bypass a restriction.

## Set up

1. Activate Gift cards and open settings.
2. Set the default name, description and expiry period. Review online purchase minimum and maximum values and the available tax settings.
3. Save, then inspect a client-facing purchase and the displayed validity information before promoting gift cards.

## Everyday use

Check the gift card and its actual payment/redemption state when helping a purchaser or recipient. Keep the offered terms clear.

## Troubleshooting

If purchase is unavailable, check app state, configured amount limits and payment availability. Do not treat a failed or pending checkout as a paid gift card.

## Limits and changes

Gift cards and service vouchers are different offerings. Tax treatment and consumer terms require the practice policy; this page does not prescribe them. Disabling the app does not automatically refund issued cards.
