# Google Analytics

Configure the analytics and conversion settings available for the public booking pages.

## Availability and permissions

Open **Settings → Apps** and find **Google Analytics**. The card's availability, price, connection state and available actions apply to your account. A help article does not activate an app. Ask the owner to review missing access; staff permissions and app availability are separate. Do not use another person's login to bypass a restriction.

## Set up

1. Open the app and connect the appropriate account where requested.
2. Review the available account, tracking ID, source URL, conversion pixel ID/label and Google Tag Manager ID fields. Use values for the intended property, not example identifiers.
3. Review the option to disable core analytics, save, and inspect the actual analytics result with an authorized test visit.

## Everyday use

Use analytics to understand the supported public-page activity and distinguish it from confirmed bookings or payments in Practize.

## Troubleshooting

If no events appear, check the saved identifiers and actual tag behavior. Older tracking fields do not prove compatibility with a current Google property; ask support when the field and provider setup do not match.

## Limits and changes

Do not add client health details or identifying information to tracking URLs or event labels. Analytics and cookie handling require a deliberate practice configuration. [Google Analytics documentation](https://developers.google.com/analytics) is the provider reference; it does not certify this integration.
