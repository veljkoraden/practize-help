# Memberships

Memberships lets a practice offer recurring paid plans to clients and track their included services or class places. A client's membership is separate from the practice's own Practize subscription.

## Activate and prepare

1. As the owner, open **Settings → Apps → Memberships**. Review the availability message. Access can depend on rollout, practice setup, package, country and a ready EasyPay payment account.
2. Complete the setup shown for your account. A purchased add-on does not bypass missing payment setup or an unavailable feature.
3. Prepare the services or open classes you want to include. Eligible services must be enabled for online booking; add-on services cannot be included. If the offering list is empty, create or enable the offerings first.
4. Open **Memberships** in the sidebar, then **Plans**. Membership management is owner-only; do not share owner credentials with reception staff.

## Create a plan

1. Select **Create plan**. Enter a clear name and description explaining what the client receives.
2. Enter a price greater than zero in the displayed currency. Choose monthly or yearly billing.
3. Choose unlimited included usage or limited usage per billing period. For limited usage, enter the number of usage units. Each included service or class place uses one unit.
4. Select at least one eligible service or class. A membership covers the selected offerings, not every service in the practice.
5. Decide whether to show the plan on public booking, then save the draft.
6. Review price, interval, allowance and every included offering before selecting **Activate plan**. Activation permanently locks these commercial terms and offerings. Create a new plan if they need to change later.

## Publish and purchase

An active plan must also be marked for public visibility to appear to clients. Online purchasing has its own availability requirements; a visible plan is not proof that checkout is enabled.

When purchasing is available, the client chooses a plan, verifies identity as requested, reviews recurring-payment consent and completes payment. **Pending payment** is not active coverage. Practize waits for verified payment confirmation. If checkout is interrupted, resume the existing checkout or use **Check payment status** rather than starting repeated purchases.

## Manage members and usage

Open **Members** to review clients, membership status, paid period and remaining allowance. Use the client's membership details when answering a coverage question. Active status alone is insufficient if the selected booking falls outside the valid paid period or is not an included offering.

A covered reservation uses its membership allowance when booked. Checking that existing reservation in through Attendance does not consume another unit. A membership-covered walk-in, where enabled, checks eligibility and capacity again when admitted. See [Attendance](https://help.practize.io/help/apps/attendance).

## Changes, cancellations and archiving

Create a replacement plan to change locked commercial terms. Archiving retains plan history and prevents editing or reactivation; it does not cancel existing subscriptions. Deactivating the Memberships app also retains data and does not cancel payment subscriptions. Review each subscription's actual cancellation state and use the available supported cancellation flow or contact support before treating future collection as stopped.

## Troubleshooting

- **No eligible services or classes:** enable online booking on a service or create/reopen a class. Remove unavailable offerings from a draft before activation.
- **Unavailable app:** follow the displayed reason rather than attempting a hidden URL.
- **Payment pending or past due:** review the current status; do not manually assume paid coverage from a checkout screenshot.
- **Allowance unavailable:** reload and inspect the member's paid period. An unavailable value does not mean unlimited usage.
- **Plan hidden:** check draft/active state and public visibility separately from purchasing availability.
- **Wrong charge or refund question:** check the actual client payment/invoice and contact the practice owner. Archiving a plan is not a refund.
