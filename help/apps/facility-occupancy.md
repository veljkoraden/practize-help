# Facility occupancy

Facility occupancy means knowing how many people are physically inside a gym, studio or other facility right now. **Live facility occupancy is not currently available.** There is no separate Occupation app to activate in Settings → Apps.

## What you can use today

1. Use [Classes and events](https://help.practize.io/help/apps/events) to configure session places and manage reservations.
2. Use [Attendance](https://help.practize.io/help/apps/attendance) to record that a reserved participant attended a particular session, or admit an eligible membership-covered walk-in where enabled.
3. Review that session's participants and attendance history for session administration.

These records answer different questions: a reservation holds a place; attendance records a visit; live occupancy would require reliable entry and exit records. Do not report the number of checked-in session participants as the number of people currently inside the building.

## What is not supported

The current reception check-in flow does not provide facility check-out, live inside/outside status, a live headcount, occupancy alerts, door unlocking or turnstile control. A QR or RFID scan is supervised identification before a staff-confirmed session check-in. It is not evidence that a person crossed a physical entrance or later left.

## Operating safely while occupancy is unavailable

Keep the facility's existing entry/exit and capacity procedures. A session's configured seat capacity still governs its reservations and applicable walk-ins, but it does not measure occupants across rooms, visitors, staff or overlapping sessions. A person attending two sessions must not be counted as two people inside.

Do not void attendance to simulate departure: voiding corrects an attendance record and changes the reported history. Do not use the Clients list's Last attendance timestamp as a live presence indicator.

## Before planning hardware

Confirm the supported reader mode and complete physical qualification before buying or rolling out a device fleet. The selected reception reader/card pair remains unqualified physically. Door controllers, exit readers and emergency-egress handling require a separate supported facility-entry design. No release date or hardware certification is implied by this help page.
