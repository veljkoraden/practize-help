# Zettle

Send supported invoices to Zettle POS and register the resulting in-person payment workflow.

## Availability and permissions

Open **Settings → Apps** and find **Zettle**. The card's availability, price, connection state and available actions apply to your account. A help article does not activate an app. Ask the owner to review missing access; staff permissions and app availability are separate. Do not use another person's login to bypass a restriction.

## Set up

1. Open Zettle activation and authorize the intended account.
2. Review settings, including whether credit notes should be used in the supported workflow.
3. Confirm that the POS connection and invoice are ready before sending an invoice to the terminal/POS workflow.
4. In an approved test setup, verify the amount and the resulting Practize invoice/payment state.

## Everyday use

Select the supported Zettle action for the invoice, complete the POS flow and check the final recorded payment. Keep the POS and invoice references available for reconciliation.

## Troubleshooting

If a POS attempt is cancelled, interrupted or uncertain, inspect its existing state before resending. Do not mark an invoice paid solely because it was sent to POS.

## Limits and changes

Zettle payment hardware is separate from the RFID reader used for Attendance. A successful authorization does not certify every terminal or refund scenario. [Official Zettle developer documentation](https://developer.zettle.com/) is the provider reference; use only the workflow exposed by Practize.
