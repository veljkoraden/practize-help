# Zapier

Connect supported Practize events to automations in other applications.

## Availability and permissions

Open **Settings → Apps** and find **Zapier**. The card's availability, price, connection state and available actions apply to your account. A help article does not activate an app. Ask the owner to review missing access; staff permissions and app availability are separate. Do not use another person's login to bypass a restriction.

## Set up

1. Activate Zapier and open its settings.
2. Select the triggers and individual fields that may be shared. Save the smallest data selection required by your workflow.
3. In Zapier, choose a supported Practize trigger and the intended destination action, then configure field mappings.
4. Test with non-sensitive data and inspect the destination result before enabling the automation.

## Everyday use

Monitor automation execution in the relevant destination and Zapier history. Recheck mappings when you change field names or the receiving system.

## Troubleshooting

For missing data, inspect the selected trigger and permitted fields. Before replaying a failed run, check whether the destination action already occurred to avoid duplicate records or messages.

## Limits and changes

App activation does not create or publish every Zap. Do not send clinical or financial information to an unauthorized destination. [Zapier documentation](https://docs.zapier.com/integrations) is the provider reference; available Practize triggers are the actual supported scope.
