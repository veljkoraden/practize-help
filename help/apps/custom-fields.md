# Custom client fields

Collect additional structured client information relevant to your practice.

## Availability and permissions

Open **Settings → Apps** and find **Custom client fields**. The card's availability, price, connection state and available actions apply to your account. A help article does not activate an app. Ask the owner to review missing access; staff permissions and app availability are separate. Do not use another person's login to bypass a restriction.

## Set up

1. Activate the app and open its settings.
2. Create the fields supported by the editor with clear labels and appropriate field types.
3. Review where each field appears and any required-field options before saving. Test on a non-sensitive example client.

## Everyday use

Open a client profile to enter or review the additional information. Use consistent meanings for fields so staff can interpret values reliably.

## Troubleshooting

If a field is missing, check its configuration, the relevant client view and your access. If validation fails, correct the value or required information instead of entering a misleading placeholder.

## Limits and changes

Do not use custom fields to bypass restricted clinical records or consent workflows. Review existing values before renaming or removing a field; a new label does not transform historic data.

Return to [App help](https://help.practize.io/help/) to find related setup guides. Before changing a connected workflow, finish pending work and verify its status in the relevant booking, invoice or client record.
