# Google Calendar

Connect a selected Google calendar to the Practize calendar workflow.

## Availability and permissions

Open **Settings → Apps** and find **Google Calendar**. The card's availability, price, connection state and available actions apply to your account. A help article does not activate an app. Ask the owner to review missing access; staff permissions and app availability are separate. Do not use another person's login to bypass a restriction.

## Set up

1. Choose Activate and sign in to the intended Google account through the authorization flow. Review the requested access.
2. Select the Google calendar to synchronize.
3. Choose whether to show Google events in Practize and whether to send Practize appointments to Google. Review per-calendar push settings, extra details and the hide-sensitive-information option.
4. Save and verify a harmless appointment in the configured direction. If using Online Booking, separately review whether Google busy events block bookable times.

## Everyday use

Maintain appointments in the appropriate originating calendar and check both sides after a change. Keep sensitive-detail settings appropriate for anyone who can see the shared Google calendar.

## Troubleshooting

If access is lost or a calendar cannot be found, reconnect the correct account and reselect the calendar. Sending appointments to Google requires write access. A provider event marked free may not block booking availability.

## Limits and changes

Before disconnecting, read the option concerning removal of Practize-originated events from Google. Synchronization does not authorize sharing confidential notes. [Google Calendar documentation](https://developers.google.com/workspace/calendar/api/guides/overview) describes provider concepts; only the options exposed by Practize are supported here.
