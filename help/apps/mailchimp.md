# Mailchimp

Connect the supported client mailing-list workflow to a selected Mailchimp audience.

## Availability and permissions

Open **Settings → Apps** and find **Mailchimp**. The card's availability, price, connection state and available actions apply to your account. A help article does not activate an app. Ask the owner to review missing access; staff permissions and app availability are separate. Do not use another person's login to bypass a restriction.

## Set up

1. Choose Activate, authorize the intended Mailchimp account and return to Practize.
2. Choose the main list/audience in settings.
3. Review whether deleting a client should unsubscribe them. Save before enabling related newsletter signup options in public booking or event settings.
4. Use a consenting test contact to confirm the correct audience and subscription state.

## Everyday use

Check the audience and actual subscription state before sending campaigns in Mailchimp. Keep mailing-list permission distinct from a client appointment or treatment consent.

## Troubleshooting

If a contact is missing, check their signup choice, selected audience and connection state. A client existing in Practize does not by itself establish a marketing subscription.

## Limits and changes

Deleting or unsubscribing has different effects; review the configured behavior first. [Mailchimp documentation](https://mailchimp.com/developer/marketing/) explains audience concepts, but only the configured Practize sharing flow applies here.
