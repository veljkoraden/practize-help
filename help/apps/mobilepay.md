# MobilePay

Connect the supported MobilePay client-payment workflow for eligible accounts.

## Availability and permissions

Open **Settings → Apps** and find **MobilePay**. The card's availability, price, connection state and available actions apply to your account. A help article does not activate an app. Ask the owner to review missing access; staff permissions and app availability are separate. Do not use another person's login to bypass a restriction.

## Set up

1. Open MobilePay activation and authorize the intended business through the supplied provider flow.
2. Return to settings and review the invoice issuer selection.
3. Check the connected state and use an approved test workflow to verify the intended invoice payment before offering it to clients.

## Everyday use

Follow the supported invoice-payment action and verify the recorded result. Use the available date-range export when reconciling the relevant MobilePay records.

## Troubleshooting

If the connection or payment cannot be confirmed, review the current state before starting another charge. Check that the invoice issuer and business account match the practice.

## Limits and changes

Availability is country- and configuration-dependent. A MobilePay connection is separate from EasyPay and Practize subscription billing. [Official MobilePay documentation](https://developer.vippsmobilepay.com/) covers provider products; not every provider product is available through this integration.

## Related help

Return to [App help](https://help.practize.io/help/) to find related setup guides. Before changing a connected workflow, finish pending work and verify its status in the relevant booking, invoice or client record.
