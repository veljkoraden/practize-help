# EasyPay

Connect the practice payment account for the supported online client-payment workflows.

## Availability and permissions

Open **Settings → Apps** and find **EasyPay**. The card's availability, price, connection state and available actions apply to your account. A help article does not activate an app. Ask the owner to review missing access; staff permissions and app availability are separate. Do not use another person's login to bypass a restriction.

## Set up

1. Open EasyPay setup as the owner. Review whether activation is available for the practice.
2. Complete the business, representative, address and payout-bank details requested by the secure onboarding flow. Review terms before accepting.
3. Return to settings and check the account readiness and any outstanding verification requirements. A submitted form is not the same as being ready to accept charges.
4. Enable and test the intended payment flow using an approved test environment before relying on it for client collections.

## Everyday use

Use the invoice or supported booking checkout and verify the final payment state in Practize. Membership activation depends on a ready account and verified payment, not merely a successful redirect.

## Troubleshooting

If payment is pending or its outcome is uncertain, inspect the existing payment and provider result before retrying or accepting another payment method. Missing verification or payout details can require owner action.

## Limits and changes

Client collections are separate from paying your Practize subscription. Deactivation does not refund transactions or cancel every recurring client subscription. [Stripe Connect documentation](https://docs.stripe.com/connect) describes the provider platform used by the supported account flow; availability remains controlled by Practize.

## Related help

Return to [App help](https://help.practize.io/help/) to find related setup guides. Before changing a connected workflow, finish pending work and verify its status in the relevant booking, invoice or client record.
