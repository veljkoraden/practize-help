# Client login

Configure client identity and account access where the legacy client-login controls are available.

## Availability and permissions

Open **Settings → Apps** and find **Client login**. The card's availability, price, connection state and available actions apply to your account. A help article does not activate an app. Ask the owner to review missing access; staff permissions and app availability are separate. Do not use another person's login to bypass a restriction.

## Set up

1. Check the current account interface first. The standalone Client login catalog entry is currently hidden; do not assume there is an activatable card.
2. Where client-login settings are exposed, review login requirements, profile-creation options and the client-facing explanation.
3. Review invoice, past-booking and cancellation access along with any cancellation deadline, then verify the public client view.

## Everyday use

Use the normal client sign-in flow and help the client recover access through supported verification. Keep booking permissions distinct from login success.

## Troubleshooting

A missing standalone card is expected under the current catalog configuration. For account access problems, verify the correct practice and client contact information.

## Limits and changes

This article documents existing client-login concepts, not a promise to expose a hidden app. Do not use staff credentials to sign in as a client.

Return to [App help](https://help.practize.io/help/) to find related setup guides. Before changing a connected workflow, finish pending work and verify its status in the relevant booking, invoice or client record.
