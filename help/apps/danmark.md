# Sygeforsikring Danmark

Submit eligible treatment information through the supported Denmark-specific workflow.

## Availability and permissions

Open **Settings → Apps** and find **Sygeforsikring Danmark**. The card's availability, price, connection state and available actions apply to your account. A help article does not activate an app. Ask the owner to review missing access; staff permissions and app availability are separate. Do not use another person's login to bypass a restriction.

## Set up

1. Activate the Danmark app where offered and review its price and settings.
2. Configure the practice and practitioner details required by setup.
3. Review the service/treatment codes and relevant client information before creating the treatment invoice.
4. Use the supported reporting flow and check the actual submission result.

## Everyday use

Keep treatment and client eligibility information correct and investigate failed reporting promptly. Use the invoice/treatment record as the source for a particular submission.

## Troubleshooting

If reporting is rejected, inspect the specific client, practitioner and treatment fields. Do not change a treatment code simply to bypass a validation error.

## Limits and changes

Submission does not guarantee reimbursement or determine client eligibility. This article describes the app workflow and is not insurance or regulatory advice.

Return to [App help](https://help.practize.io/help/) to find related setup guides. Before changing a connected workflow, finish pending work and verify its status in the relevant booking, invoice or client record.
