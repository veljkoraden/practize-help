# Secure messaging

Exchange secure messages with clients through the supported Practize messaging flow.

## Availability and permissions

Open **Settings → Apps** and find **Secure messaging**. The card's availability, price, connection state and available actions apply to your account. A help article does not activate an app. Ask the owner to review missing access; staff permissions and app availability are separate. Do not use another person's login to bypass a restriction.

## Set up

1. Activate Secure messaging and review its displayed price and settings.
2. Configure the away status and away message when needed.
3. Open the correct client and use the secure messaging action. Verify the recipient before sending.

## Everyday use

Review conversations and reply through the secure channel. Keep the away message current so clients understand when to expect a response.

## Troubleshooting

If a message cannot be sent or accessed, check client contact information, app state and your permissions. A notification email is not itself proof that the client read the secure message.

## Limits and changes

Do not use an ordinary email or SMS body as a substitute for protected message content. This app does not guarantee emergency monitoring or immediate responses.
