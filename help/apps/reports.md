# Reports

Access additional reporting for practice performance.

## Availability and permissions

Open **Settings → Apps** and find **Reports**. The card's availability, price, connection state and available actions apply to your account. A help article does not activate an app. Ask the owner to review missing access; staff permissions and app availability are separate. Do not use another person's login to bypass a restriction.

## Set up

1. Activate Reports and open its settings.
2. Review configured report recipients and enter only authorized email addresses.
3. Open the relevant report and choose its available date and scope filters before interpreting the results.

## Everyday use

Use the report period, practice timezone and applied filters when comparing totals. Inspect underlying records when a number needs explanation.

## Troubleshooting

If a total differs from expectations, check filters, dates and record states such as cancelled or unpaid. A permission restriction can also change which data is visible.

## Limits and changes

Reports are not live facility occupancy. A booking count, payment total and attendance count measure different things. Do not distribute client-level reports to unauthorized recipients.

## Related help

Return to [App help](https://help.practize.io/help/) to find related setup guides. Before changing a connected workflow, finish pending work and verify its status in the relevant booking, invoice or client record.
