# EHF electronic invoicing

Configure supported EHF invoices for Norwegian business and public-sector recipients.

## Availability and permissions

Open **Settings → Apps** and find **EHF electronic invoicing**. The card's availability, price, connection state and available actions apply to your account. A help article does not activate an app. Ask the owner to review missing access; staff permissions and app availability are separate. Do not use another person's login to bypass a restriction.

## Set up

1. Activate EHF where it is available and review the displayed charge.
2. Open settings and select the payment terms and payment means exposed by the EHF configuration.
3. Check sender, recipient and invoice reference details for the intended customer.
4. Use the supported invoice submission flow and inspect the result rather than assuming configuration alone delivered it.

## Everyday use

Review the invoice and electronic-delivery state when following up with a recipient. Keep payment reconciliation separate from transmission.

## Troubleshooting

For rejected or incomplete submissions, use the error to correct the recipient, references or payment details. Confirm whether an earlier submission exists before repeating it.

## Limits and changes

EHF is not an automatic payment collection method. The app is country-dependent and does not establish that every recipient can accept the submitted document.
