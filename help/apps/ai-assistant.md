# AI Assistant

Ask supported help questions and permitted read-only questions through the Help assistant. Help articles remain available when assistant access is unavailable.

## Availability and permissions

Open **Settings → Apps** and find **AI Assistant**. The card's availability, price, connection state and available actions apply to your account. A help article does not activate an app. Ask the owner to review missing access; staff permissions and app availability are separate. Do not use another person's login to bypass a restriction.

## Set up

1. Open the AI Assistant card in Apps and review its pilot and purchase availability.
2. Where offered, complete the supported AI Starter purchase and wait for confirmed access. Activation and confirmed billing are separate.
3. Review the assistant settings and privacy explanation, then open the assistant from Help.
4. Start with one specific question, such as how to use a feature. For supported data questions, include only the context needed to identify the request.

## Everyday use

Review the answer and its source/context before relying on it. Ask one read-only question at a time; use the normal app workflow to make changes.

## Troubleshooting

If a request is pending, open the latest conversation rather than submitting again. Use **Retry the same request** only when submission is unconfirmed. For a provider-outcome-unknown state, do not resend; check later. Budget or provider errors do not prevent reading Help Articles.

## Limits and changes

The assistant does not grant access to restricted client or clinical data, and cannot promise unsupported actions. Deactivation stops use but does not cancel billing; manage the subscription separately. Never include secrets or unnecessary personal details.

## Related help

Return to [App help](https://help.practize.io/help/) to find related setup guides. Before changing a connected workflow, finish pending work and verify its status in the relevant booking, invoice or client record.
