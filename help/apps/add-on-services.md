# Add-on services

Offer additional services alongside a primary booking.

## Availability and permissions

Open **Settings → Apps** and find **Add-on services**. The card's availability, price, connection state and available actions apply to your account. A help article does not activate an app. Ask the owner to review missing access; staff permissions and app availability are separate. Do not use another person's login to bypass a restriction.

## Set up

1. Activate Add-on services.
2. Open service management and configure the additional offerings using the available add-on options.
3. Check the related main services, price and timing, then preview a booking that should offer the add-on.

## Everyday use

Review the final booking and charge when clients select extras. Keep service descriptions clear about what is optional.

## Troubleshooting

If an add-on is missing, verify its availability and association with the selected service. Check the final appointment duration and price rather than assuming the extra was included.

## Limits and changes

Add-on services are excluded from Memberships offering selection. Activating this app does not make every extra free or automatically covered by a plan.

## Related help

Return to [App help](https://help.practize.io/help/) to find related setup guides. Before changing a connected workflow, finish pending work and verify its status in the relevant booking, invoice or client record.
