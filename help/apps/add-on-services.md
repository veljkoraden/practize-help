# Add-on services

## What an add-on service is

An add-on is an optional item attached to an eligible main service. It can extend a booking with extra time, content, or price. It is not a standalone bookable service unless Practize explicitly presents it that way.

## Create and attach an add-on

Open the services area and create the add-on item. Give it a clear client-facing name, price, VAT treatment, and any duration or availability settings shown. Then open each main service that should offer the add-on and attach it.

Activate **Add-on services** from **Settings → Apps** when the practice requires that App. Because the App has no separate settings page in some layouts, configuration may happen on the service itself.

## Verify the booking flow

Preview the public booking page as a client. Select an eligible service and confirm that the intended add-ons appear, that optional and required behavior is clear, and that the final duration and price are correct before confirmation. Also check the appointment and invoice after a test booking.

If an add-on is missing, check that it is active, attached to the selected service, supported by that booking/payment flow, and visible for the chosen calendar or location. Existing bookings normally keep their recorded line details; changing an add-on should not be assumed to rewrite past bookings.

## Permissions and safe use

Only users allowed to manage services should create, edit, or remove add-ons. Avoid names or descriptions containing client-specific or sensitive information. The AI Assistant can explain setup from this article but does not modify services or bookings.
