# Client tags

Organize client profiles with labels for filtering and everyday coordination.

## Availability and permissions

Open **Settings → Apps** and find **Client tags**. The card's availability, price, connection state and available actions apply to your account. A help article does not activate an app. Ask the owner to review missing access; staff permissions and app availability are separate. Do not use another person's login to bypass a restriction.

## Set up

1. Activate Client tags and open its settings.
2. Create or review the available tags using short, clear names agreed with your team.
3. Assign the relevant tag to a client and verify it appears on the intended profile and filtering surface.

## Everyday use

Use tags to find relevant groups of clients. Keep assignments current when a client situation changes.

## Troubleshooting

If a client is missing from filtered results, remove other filters and check the actual tag assignment. Two similarly named tags can represent different groups; agree on one vocabulary.

## Limits and changes

Tags do not grant permissions, prove consent or activate a membership. Avoid unnecessary sensitive information in tag names visible to staff.
