# Client records

Keep structured notes and records on the correct client profile.

## Availability and permissions

Open **Settings → Apps** and find **Client records**. The card's availability, price, connection state and available actions apply to your account. A help article does not activate an app. Ask the owner to review missing access; staff permissions and app availability are separate. Do not use another person's login to bypass a restriction.

## Set up

1. Activate the Records/Journal app and review its settings.
2. Review the option to lock all journals and understand its effect before saving. Assign only the necessary record access to staff.
3. Open a test client record and verify the note workflow, authorship and available locking controls.

## Everyday use

Check the client identity before writing a note, save the record and verify it is stored. Follow the practice procedure for locking and correcting records.

## Troubleshooting

If editing is unavailable, check locking state and permissions. Do not copy restricted records into ordinary client fields to bypass access rules.

## Limits and changes

Clinical-record access is separate from general client access. App activation does not establish a legal retention policy or authorize sharing with external integrations.

## Related help

Return to [App help](https://help.practize.io/help/) to find related setup guides. Before changing a connected workflow, finish pending work and verify its status in the relevant booking, invoice or client record.
